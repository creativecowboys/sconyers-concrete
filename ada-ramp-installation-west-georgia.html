Regulations

ADA Ramp Dimensional Requirements

Key constraints checked on every accessibility install from the 2010 ADA Design Standards.

ADA Standards for Accessible Design Quick Reference

  • Maximum running slope: 1:12 (8.33% maximum grade)
  • Maximum cross slope: 1:48 (2.08% maximum grade)
  • Minimum clear width: 36 inches between handrails
  • Maximum rise before landing: 30 inches of rise
  • Landing minimum size: 60 inches by 60 inches square
  • Detectable warnings: Required on curb ramps, 24 inches deep

We build to these standards on every ramp. If your site has constraints that affect compliance (existing grades, limited space, historic-property restrictions), we'll flag them in the estimate and propose code-compliant solutions.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about ADA ramp installation and compliance in West Georgia.

What's the current ADA ramp slope requirement?

The maximum running slope for a ramp in new construction is 1:12 (8.33% grade). Any slope steeper than 1:20 (5%) is considered a ramp and must follow all ADA ramp requirements (landings, handrails).

How long does an ADA ramp installation take?

A typical ramp pour or retrofit takes 2–4 days of on-site work. This includes demolition of existing concrete, base prep, forming, reinforcement, pouring, and detectable warning installation.

Do you handle the detectable warning surface installation?

Yes. We install compliant truncated dome tiles or mats (cast-in-place or surface-applied) in the required size, contrast color, and placement.

Can you do ADA work on an occupied property with minimal disruption?

Yes. We frequently work on active commercial properties, phasing our pours and placing temporary pedestrian routes or doing off-hours work to keep your doors open.

What's the cost of a typical ADA ramp installation?

Costs vary widely depending on whether it's new construction or a retrofit (which requires demolition, slope correction, and utility adjustments). Demolition and base correction add labor, but a standard retrofit typically runs between $3,500 and $8,000 depending on size and handrail needs. We provide free detailed estimates.

Will the ramp pass inspection the first time?

Yes. We build strictly to the 2010 ADA Standards. We check our forms and slopes before and during the pour to ensure they pass inspection.

Do you do ADA remediation work driven by DOJ complaints or property assessments?

Yes. GCs and property managers often call us after a facility assessment or code violation. We will tear out the non-compliant ramp and replace it with a fully compliant one.

What cities do you serve for ADA ramp work?

We serve all of West Georgia, including Douglasville, Newnan, Carrollton, Villa Rica, LaGrange, Dallas, Hiram, and surrounding areas.
